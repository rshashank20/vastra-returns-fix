# Resume bullets — The Returns Fix (Vastra)

- Modeled the fully-loaded cost of returns for a D2C fashion brand: ₹511 per return vs the ₹88 leadership assumed (₹18.2cr/yr). Triangulated true return reasons from claimed reasons × QC results × order patterns — 47.6% fit/remorse, 10.2% fake damage claims, 8% bracketing — and designed a segmented fee + exchange-first policy projected at +₹3.5cr/yr net margin (modeled).
- Mined 2.1M return-journey events and found the refund bottleneck: Monday-batch QC (5.1 vs 2.9 days on breached SLAs), with late refunds driving 3.2x support tickets. Wrote the BRD, as-is/to-be flows, 10 user stories and 8 UAT cases for the returns-module rebuild (payback under a month, modeled).
