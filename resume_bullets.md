# Resume bullets — The Returns Fix (Vastra)

- Analyzed 356K returns to estimate the fully-loaded cost at ₹511/return vs ₹88 previously assumed (~₹18.2 Cr/year); combined customer reason, QC outcome and ordering behaviour to identify fit/remorse (47.6%), QC-resellable damage claims (10.2%) and bracketing (8.0%), informing a segmented fee + exchange-first policy.
- Analyzed 2.1M return-journey events to identify QC as a major refund-delay driver (5.1 days for SLA-breached returns vs 2.9 days for clean returns); linked breaches to 3.2× higher WISMO contacts and translated findings into BRD v1.0, AS-IS/TO-BE flows, 10 user stories and 8 UAT cases for the returns-process rebuild.
