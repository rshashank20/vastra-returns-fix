## returns (357,009 rows)

Contains ~0.4% undiscovered duplicate app-retry rows — true dupes share (order_id, line_id). pickup_attempts: 1-3 (courier failure model).

- `return_id` (object)
- `line_id` (object)
- `order_id` (object)
- `sku` (object)
- `size` (object)
- `return_date` (datetime64[ns])
